CRIMSON DUSK v1.1.0 MULTIPLAYER FOUNDATION

This branch begins multiplayer without duplicating Bandit art.

PLAYER 1
- Existing controls and gameplay remain unchanged.

PLAYER 2 LOCAL TEST CONTROLS
- J / L: move left / right
- I: jump / double jump
- K: crouch
- P: directional dash

ART ARCHITECTURE
- Both players reference the same Bandit IFP assets.
- Player 2 is recolored at runtime and cached per animation frame.
- Only crimson/red-dominant pixels shift toward wine-purple.
- Gold trim, black clothing, weapons, skin, and silhouette are preserved.
- No duplicate Player 2 sprite sheets are included.

CURRENT SCOPE
- This is the multiplayer entity/render/input foundation, not finished network multiplayer.
- Player 2 movement, jump, crouch, dash, animation state, and palette identity are active.
- Combat, damage targeting, revive/death, shared camera rules, room transitions, UI, and networking still need to be generalized from Player 1 to both entities.
