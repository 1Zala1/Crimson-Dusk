Crimson Dusk v1.2.0 Final Co-op Candidate

Required release rules:
- Bandit II reuses Bandit I IFP assets with runtime burgundy palette shift.
- Multiplayer HP multiplier: 1.20 for all spawned mobs and bosses.
- Every normal enemy draws an overhead HP bar.
- Named bosses retain full-screen boss HP bars.
- Enemies, Hive Mother and Crimson Lord select the nearest living Bandit.
- Harlequin moon projectiles select living Bandits.
- Projectiles can collide with either player.
- P2 has melee, ranged, shotgun, flask, stance, crouch, jump and directional dash controls.
- Room transitions reposition Bandit II with Bandit I.
- Team wipe returns to checkpoint; a single fallen Bandit auto-recovers beside the survivor at 50% HP.
- Existing single-player content and save migration remain intact.
